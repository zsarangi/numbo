Numbo

Every day, ten sums. Answer them as fast as you can, earn a medal for your time, and send it to someone who thinks they're quicker.

Type 98 and the next sum appears straight away. There's no Enter key to press.

How a round works

Pick a level and press Start. The clock runs until the tenth sum is answered. A wrong answer costs nothing except the time you spend fixing it.

If one has you stuck, Skip moves you on and adds 10 seconds to your time. Skips show up in your result, so a clean run is always the better look.

Three levels

  • Easy — times tables to 10 × 10, and adding or taking away a single digit.
  • Medium — tables to 15 × 15, and two-digit adding and subtracting.
  • Hard — multiplying up to 15 × 50, and three-digit adding and subtracting.

Every level has five add-or-subtract sums and five multiply-or-divide, shuffled. Divisions always come out whole, and subtractions never go below zero. Each level is its own game: play one or all three, and each keeps its own streak and record.

Medals

  • 💎 Platinum — under 30 seconds.
  • 🥇 Gold — under 45 seconds.
  • 🥈 Silver — under 60 seconds.
  • 🥉 Bronze — under 90 seconds.

The times are the same on every level. That's deliberate: the sums get harder, the medals don't get easier, so platinum on Hard is something to shout about.

The row of squares in your result shows how each sum went:

  • ⚡ Platinum pace, under 3 seconds.
  • 🟩 Gold pace.
  • 🟨 Silver pace.
  • 🟧 Bronze pace.
  • 🟥 Slower than that.
  • ⏭️ Skipped.

A new puzzle at midnight

The sums change at midnight UK time, and everyone gets the same ten on the same day. You get one timed attempt per level per day, and the clock keeps running if you leave the page mid-round. Missed a few days? Past puzzles are behind the clock button.

Some days will be kinder than others. The numbers are drawn at random, so that's luck, and it's the same luck for everybody.

Challenge a friend

"Challenge a friend" copies your result with a link. The link carries your time, so whoever opens it plays the same ten sums on the same level and is told who won.

Your stats

Streaks and results are kept in your browser, on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere, there's no account, and clearing your browser data clears your record too. Play on your phone and your laptop and you'll have two separate streaks.

How to play

Each day there are ten sums to answer as fast as you can. Press Start, type each answer, and the next sum appears the moment you get it right. There's no Enter key to press and no penalty for a wrong answer beyond the time it costs you.

Stuck? Skip moves you on, and adds 10 seconds to your time.

Levels

  • EasyTimes tables to 10 × 10, and adding or taking away a single digit.
  • MediumTables to 15 × 15, and two-digit adding and subtracting.
  • HardUp to 15 × 50, and three-digit adding and subtracting.

Every level has five add-or-subtract sums and five multiply-or-divide, in a shuffled order. Each level is its own game, with its own time, streak and record.

Medals

  • 💎Platinum — under 30 seconds.
  • 🥇Gold — under 45.
  • 🥈Silver — under 60.
  • 🥉Bronze — under 90.

The times are the same on every level, so a medal on Hard is worth more than the same one on Easy.

The daily puzzle

New sums at midnight UK time, the same ones for everybody. You get one timed attempt per level per day, and the clock keeps running if you leave the page. Missed a few? The clock button opens past puzzles.

Challenge a friend

Share your result and the link carries your time with it. Whoever opens it plays the same sums and sees who won.

Your results are stored in this browser only — no account, nothing sent anywhere.
